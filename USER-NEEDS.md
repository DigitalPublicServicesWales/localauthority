# User needs for the funded childcare page (working hypotheses)

These candidate needs are inferred from the supplied content pattern. They are **not yet validated research findings**.

## 1. Understand what funded childcare is available

As a… a parent or carer of a 2-year-old

I need to… understand what funded childcare is available

So that I can… I can decide whether it could help my family

Related page content: What you can get — hours, term-time availability and possible additional costs.

## 2. Know when my child can use it

As a… a parent or carer

I need to… know when my child can start and stop receiving funded childcare

So that I can… I can plan childcare arrangements

Related page content: When your child can get funded childcare.

## 3. Find a suitable provider

As a… a parent or carer

I need to… find a childcare provider that offers the funded hours in my area

So that I can… I can arrange a place for my child

Related page content: Where you can use the funded hours; find a provider link; before-you-apply guidance.

## 4. Prepare and apply

As a… a parent or carer

I need to… know how to apply and what information I need

So that I can… I can submit an application without avoidable delays

Related page content: Before you apply; documents to upload; application link.

## 5. Know what happens after applying

As a… a parent or carer

I need to… know what happens after I apply and when my child can start

So that I can… I know what to expect and what to do next

Related page content: After you apply; confirmation and next steps. The wording depends on the application variant.

## 6. Get help if I need it

As a… a parent or carer

I need to… know who to contact about applying or finding childcare

So that I can… I can get help if I cannot complete the next step myself

Related page content: Contact us — application support and Family Information Service.

## Page boundaries

Do not duplicate other funding schemes, provider directories, application services or provider administration guidance. Do not make eligibility decisions.
